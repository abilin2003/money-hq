# Upgrade Phase 2: simpler navigation and a new Overview

Done 26 Nov 2026.

## Navigation
Five sections, each with a row of tabs for its pages. Every page keeps its address, so old links still work.

| Section | Pages |
|---|---|
| Overview | Overview |
| Analytics | Spending (new), Trends, History, Transactions |
| Planning | Payday plan, Budgets, Goals, Bills (moved out of Budgets), Money day |
| Wealth | Wealth (accounts, net worth, assets and liabilities) |
| Activity | Review inbox, IOUs, Import |

Settings stays separate (sidebar on a laptop, top bar on a phone). Add transaction is a button in the sidebar, or
**+** in the phone's top bar. The old phone "More" page is replaced by the sections (`#more` opens the Overview).

## Overview
- **Summary cards:** safe to spend (with the cash check), spent vs budget, saved and invested, net worth.
- **Main:** an interactive spending vs budget chart (point, tap or use the arrow keys to read any day), buckets
  with progress bars, next payday and upcoming bills, alerts, one insight and recent transactions.
- **Phone:** its own order, not the laptop's columns stacked: summary cards (safe to spend full width), alerts
  (only when there are any), buckets, payday and bills, chart, recent lines, insight.

## Moved, not removed
- The full breakdown, bucket table with last cycle, categories vs last cycle and the spending calendar →
  **Analytics → Spending**, which also adds shops and payees and works for any cycle in your history.
- Money in vs out per cycle → **Trends** (it was already there).
- Where your pay goes (the flow chart) → **Payday plan**, drawn from the plan as you edit it.
- Bills → their own **Planning → Bills** tab, with what's coming up in the next 31 days.
- Transactions now covers any cycle in your history, not just the current records.
