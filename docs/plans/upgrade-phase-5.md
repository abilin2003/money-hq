# Upgrade Phase 5: payday planning, budgets, goals and wealth

Done 27 Nov 2026.

Planning's tabs are now: Payday plan, Money day, Budgets, Goals, Manage (Manage has a Bills tab; `#bills` still works).

## Budgets (overview) and Manage (set-up)
- **Budgets** shows, for any cycle: allocated, rolled over, spent, left, pace (where even spending would put you
  today), projected (spent ÷ days gone × days in the cycle) and warnings (over budget, or on course to go over).
- **A budget cut on purpose is respected**: a budget lower than last cycle's is labelled "Cut on purpose from £X to
  £Y" and isn't flagged as "too low" (on Budgets or the Overview); only the pace and projection can warn about it.
- **Manage** keeps category mappings, rollover (sweep or roll over), essential buckets, the reserve and bills.

## Payday plan and Money day, connected
- Money day is seven steps: import, reconcile balances, review last cycle (with links to Analytics), update savings
  and wealth, check IOUs, plan this payday, confirm allocations. Steps tick themselves when Money HQ can see
  they're done.
- Every plan line is **planned**, **completed** (a matching payment, same kind and amount, money going out, is in
  your bank lines this cycle) or **verified** (you confirmed it on Money day), shown separately on both pages.
- A plan copied from last cycle doesn't carry over last cycle's confirmations.

## Goals
Cards with the current balance, target, % done, deadline, what each payday needs, expected finish date (at your
planned amount, or your recent pace if none), a progress history chart, and the linked account or pot. Sinking-fund
balances now keep a history (each time you update one on the Payday plan) so pot-linked goals show progress.

## Wealth
Summary tiles with when each was last updated; net worth, assets and liabilities over time; cash vs investments
(and pension) over time; investments put in vs gained (value now − value then − money put in since); any
account's balance history; and one form to update every balance at once.
Only account balances are added up: sinking funds are pots inside the savings account and transfers move money
between your own accounts, so neither is counted twice (tested).
